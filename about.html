Enterprise B2B Partner

Engineering Consistency in Bulk Custom Apparel.

Vanguard Textile Co. operates as a specialized B2B apparel production and custom decoration partner. We serve commercial enterprises, institutional faculties, sports federations, and creative merchandise companies who require strict batch uniformity, verifiable fabric weights, and transparent wholesale execution.

Production Infrastructure

Integrated Manufacturing Capabilities

Our facilities bring circular yarn knitting, precision computerized pattern cutting, and industrial screen printing under unified quality oversight.

Knitting & Fabric Milling

By sourcing long-staple combed cotton and high-filament micro polyesters directly from partner spinning mills, we maintain total control over yarn counts, loop density, and gram-per-square-meter (GSM) accuracy.

  • ✓ 180 to 260 GSM single jerseys for tees and oversized fits
  • ✓ 240 GSM anti-curl honeycomb pique for executive polo collars
  • ✓ 320 to 340 GSM 3-thread combed fleece and unbrushed French terry
  • ✓ AeroDry capillary micro-mesh for moisture-wicking jerseys

Industrial Print & Stitch Floor

Our embellishment floor is engineered for high throughput without sacrificing registration fidelity. Whether deploying water-based discharge inks for breathable softness or multi-head Tajima embroidery, every placement is locked to CAD alignment fixtures.

  • ✓ Carousel automated screen presses supporting up to 8 spot colors
  • ✓ Direct-to-Film (DTF) & Direct-to-Garment (DTG) photographic stations
  • ✓ High-tensile polyester and rayon embroidery thread vaults
  • ✓ Continuous roll rotary sublimation calendars for athletic jerseys
Zero Compromise

Quality Assurance Benchmarks

Every garment leaving our packing floor is verified against standard commercial tolerances to eliminate surprises in your bulk distribution.

Shrinkage Stability

All single jersey and pique fabrics undergo pre-shrinking and heat-setting compaction, maintaining dimensional shrinkage strictly under 3.5% across standard commercial laundering.

Azo-Free Colorfastness

Utilizing certified reactive dyes, our fabrics achieve ratings of 4 to 5 for wash fastness, rub fastness, and perspiration resistance, ensuring vivid brand identity retention.

Reinforced Seam Architecture

Double-needle coverstitching on cuffs and hems, paired with shoulder-to-shoulder internal collar herringbone neck taping, prevents seam blowouts and collar distortion.

Guiding Philosophy

Mission, Vision & Partner Commitment

Our Core Mission

To provide corporate organizations, educational institutions, and brand teams with dependable apparel manufacturing where fabric hand-feel, true-to-spec weights, and precision embellishments remain consistent from the initial prototype sample through repeat 10,000-unit production runs.

Our Manufacturing Vision

To modernize commercial textile procurement by combining hands-on garment craftsmanship with streamlined digital tools — enabling procurement managers to configure complex size matrices, inspect fabric spec sheets, and receive transparent quote schedules in minutes.
