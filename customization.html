Standard Placement Coordinates

Print & Embroidery Placement Guide

Our laser-aligned platen systems adhere to strict industrial placement coordinates.

Zone A

Left Chest Crest

Standard: 3.5" to 4.2" width. Positioned 3" below collar seam. Ideal for corporate logos and embroidered monograms.

Screen • Embroidery
Zone B

Full Front Center

Standard: 10" to 12" width x 14" height. Centered across chest. Ideal for campus festivals and brand statements.

Zone C

Upper Shoulder Back

Left / Right Sleeve

Pre-Flight Artwork Preparation

Artwork Requirements for Bulk Production

Supported File Formats

Vector formats are strongly preferred for sharp screen separation: .AI, .EPS, .PDF, .SVG. High-resolution raster artwork (.PNG, .TIFF) must have a transparent background and minimum 300 DPI at actual print size.

Pantone Matching System (PMS)

Please supply Pantone Solid Coated (C) color codes for screen printing. Inks are hand-weighed against Pantone formulas under certified 5000K daylight lighting.

Don't have vector artwork? Our in-house prepress team will assist in vectorizing low-resolution logos for production screen separation once your bulk order enquiry is submitted.
