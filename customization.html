Factory Decoration Studio

Precision Apparel Embellishment & Branding

We house six dedicated commercial print and embroidery lines. Each decoration technique is matched to optimal fabric compositions, order volumes, and performance environments to guarantee enduring visual impact.

Best for 50+ Units

01 • Screen Printing

The gold standard for high-volume corporate merchandise and event apparel. Uses automated squeegee carousel presses pushing calibrated plastisol or soft-hand water-based discharge inks through high-mesh count screens.

Hand-Feel: Soft-to-Medium Durability: 60+ Washes
Photographic & Low MOQ

02 • Direct-to-Garment (DTG)

High-resolution digital inkjet heads spray water-based pigments directly into 100% combed cotton fibers. Ideal for complex multi-tonal photographic illustrations, color gradients, and limited edition art runs.

Hand-Feel: Ultra Breathable Resolution: Up to 1200 DPI
All Fabric Compatible

03 • Direct-to-Film (DTF)

Digitally printed onto specialized PET film, backed with hot-melt polyurethane adhesive powder, and thermally cured. Yields razor-sharp micro-details, ultra-vibrant colors, and exceptional stretch memory.

Hand-Feel: Smooth Film Elasticity: High Stretch
100% AeroDry Polyester

04 • Dye Sublimation

Under high temperature and pressure, sublimation inks transition from solid to gas, bonding molecularly with synthetic polyester filaments. Unbeatable for all-over marathon jerseys and team kits.

Hand-Feel: Zero Ink Weight Permanence: Never Fades/Cracks
Specialty Finishes

05 • Heat Transfer & Silicone

Engineered for metallic foils, high-visibility reflective workwear strips, and raised 3D silicone chest badges. Adds distinctive tactile dimensions to executive apparel and safety uniforms.

Finish: Metallic / Reflective / Matte Best on: Uniforms & Jackets
Polos, Hoodies & Jackets

06 • High-Density Embroidery

High-speed Tajima multi-head industrial embroidery machines driving colorfast German polyester thread. Available with 3D foam underlay puff for dimensional chest crests and executive sleeve monograms.

Hand-Feel: Textured Relief Longevity: Lifetime of Garment

Standard Placement Zones

Print & Embroidery Placement Guide

Our laser-aligned platen systems adhere to strict industrial placement coordinates.

Zone A

Left Chest Crest

Standard: 3.5" to 4.2" width. Positioned 3" below the collar seam. Ideal for corporate logos and embroidered monograms.

Screen • Embroidery
Zone B

Full Front Center

Standard: 10" to 12" width x 14" height. Centered across chest. Ideal for campus festivals and high-impact brand statements.

Screen • DTF • DTG
Zone C

Upper Shoulder Back

Standard: 11" to 14" width. Positioned across the upper scapula. Ideal for conference staff identifiers and event itineraries.

Screen • DTF • Puff
Zone D

Left / Right Sleeve

Standard: 3" to 3.5" width. Centered on outer bicep hem. Ideal for secondary partner logos, flags, and year stamps.

Screen • Embroidery
Pre-Flight Artwork Preparation

Artwork Requirements for Bulk Production

Supported File Formats

Vector formats are strongly preferred for sharp screen separation: .AI, .EPS, .PDF, .SVG. High-resolution raster artwork (.PNG, .TIFF) must have a transparent background and minimum 300 DPI at actual print size.

Pantone Matching System (PMS)

Please supply Pantone Solid Coated (C) color codes for screen printing. Inks are hand-weighed against Pantone formulas under certified 5000K daylight lighting.

Don't have vector artwork? Our in-house prepress team will assist in vectorizing low-resolution logos for production screen separation once your bulk order enquiry is submitted.
